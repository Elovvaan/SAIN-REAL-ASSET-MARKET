FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Common support questions

How do I add funds to my Asset Vault?

Sign in, open My Asset Vault, and choose Add Outside Funds. A funding instruction does not increase your available balance until the outside payment is confirmed.

Where can I find a payment or funding reference?

Open My Asset Vault and review funding instructions or recorded activity. Keep the instruction ID, invoice ID, transaction hash, or external payment reference available.

Why is a payment still pending?

Pending means the external payment, blockchain confirmation, settlement step, or platform verification has not completed. Pending amounts are not included in the recorded available balance.

Does SRA ask for my crypto seed phrase or password?

No. SRA support should never ask for a wallet seed phrase, private key, password, or one-time authentication code.
